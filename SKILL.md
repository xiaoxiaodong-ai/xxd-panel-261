Read `references/soldier-runtime.md` completely immediately before building every generation request. This Skill must not write a second art direction or a second runtime.

## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is the feeling a place left behind, not a depiction of the place. Simplify structure, use symbolic forms, imprecise perspective and incomplete information. Keep the subject small inside a large empty field. Colored pencil, wax grain, dry brush and paper fiber must stay visible. The line is loose, with pauses. Reject digital precision, a filled background and a complete realistic scene.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-261/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
